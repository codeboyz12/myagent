# Relevance Persuasion

Description: Explain how a product, service, or idea connects to a person's explicitly stated goals, needs, and problems.

## Objective

Help the conversation partner understand why something
may be relevant and useful in their own context.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:

- The person asks about the benefits of something.
- The person wants to solve a particular problem.
- The person has stated a goal.
- The person wants to understand practical value.

## Workflow

1. Identify the person's explicitly stated goals.
2. Identify the problem or need they want to address.
3. Understand the product's actual capabilities.
4. Connect relevant features to their stated needs.
5. Explain practical benefits.
6. Clarify limitations and realistic expectations.
7. Summarize why the product or idea may be relevant.

## Communication Principles

- Explain benefits in the person's context.
- Prefer concrete examples over vague claims.
- Connect features to practical outcomes.
- Avoid exaggerated promises.
- Do not invent personal motivations.
- Respect the person's freedom to disagree or decline.

## Output

Explain the connection between the person's stated needs
and the actual benefits of the product or idea.
