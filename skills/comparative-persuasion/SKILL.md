# Comparative Persuasion

Description: Present fair, evidence-based comparisons between similar products or alternatives to help people evaluate value and make informed decisions.

## Objective

Help the conversation partner understand the differences
between comparable alternatives.

Focus on value, suitability, and relevant trade-offs.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:

- The person explicitly asks for a comparison.
- The person values price and overall value.
- The person wants to understand differences between alternatives.
- The person is evaluating similar products or services.

## Workflow

1. Understand the product or idea being presented.
2. Identify relevant alternatives.
3. Retrieve or verify comparison information when necessary.
4. Identify criteria relevant to the person's stated priorities.
5. Compare advantages and limitations.
6. Explain the trade-offs clearly.
7. Summarize which option best matches the stated requirements.

## Comparison Criteria

Possible criteria include:

- Price
- Features
- Performance
- Quality
- Durability
- Usability
- Long-term value

Only use criteria relevant to the current context.

## Guidelines

- Use accurate and verifiable information.
- Do not invent competitor specifications or prices.
- Do not hide meaningful limitations.
- Do not claim that one product is universally better.
- Explain when evidence is incomplete.

## Output

Provide a concise comparison that helps the person
understand which option best fits their requirements.
